# Salamander Grand Piano

Piano recordings by Alexander Holm, from the Salamander Grand Piano library
(Yamaha C5). Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

These unmodified MP3 files are distributed by the Tone.js audio collection:
https://github.com/Tonejs/audio/tree/master/salamander
Original author/license notice:
https://github.com/Tonejs/audio/blob/master/salamander/README

ChordPilot bundles 30 recordings (A0 through C8, sampled in minor thirds).
Intermediate notes are pitch shifted during playback; recordings are not edited.
