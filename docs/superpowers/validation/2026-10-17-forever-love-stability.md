The handover correctly identified a roughly 40-second stability region and many A-family raw detections overwritten by D-family labels in Verse 1. The decisive mechanism is more specific than the transition penalty alone: `region_roots[region]` could add the sustained-mode **+1.6 emission bonus to a D-root candidate in every cell** of that region, even where the cell had no matching measured bass. The region from 0.395–40.320 s had only 9 clear independent-bass references among 177 cells. Thus a root associated with the noisy opening could be rewarded throughout the electric-piano verse.

The fix applies that region-root bonus only where a cell also has a clear matching bass reference **when the region spans more than four bars and fewer than 20% of its cells have clear independent-bass evidence**. Existing scoring remains unchanged in bass-rich regions. It does not infer a chord from the score, suppress the clock intro, or change the selected tempo.
