# Second chord-vocabulary batch

The chord parser, candidate formulas, keyboard/staff tones, transposition, session strings and MusicXML export now support `9#11`, `13#11`, `13b9`, `mmaj9`, `7b9#11` and `7#9#11`, including note-name inversions. `m(maj9)` and `mM9` normalize to `mmaj9`. PyChord supplies the requested dominant formulas; `mmaj9` uses the conventional 1–♭3–5–7–9 formula because that PyChord quality is unavailable. Existing `7#5` and `7b13` remain distinct.

The interrupted initial implementation made `9#11` win on diffuse accompaniment where the established major or half-diminished result was better. The full suite exposed 63 failing Python assertions, including all-key half-diminished checks. Automatic proposal of the six new complex qualities now requires **each formula pitch class to contribute at least 10% of the measured accompaniment chroma**. This is a conservative eligibility gate, not a measured universal threshold. Manual notation, parsing and export remain available even when audio support is too weak to propose the symbol. Clear synthetic formulas still win in all twelve roots; a recorded diffuse Gb-major vector no longer offers the spurious Db9#11 candidate.

After the gate and cache-version update, the full suite passed (315 JavaScript, 205 Python tests). All **83 selected real-audio Local Study predictions were identical** to the preceding v27 confidence reports: Hero 17/20 roots, 11 functions, 9 exact; Virtual Insanity 7/13, 4, 1; Gravity 12/16, 10, 3; Overjoyed 19/21, 13, 2; September 5/13, 1, 0. The current reports are ignored `.chordpilot-data/benchmarks/reference-reports/*-v28.json`. Cache versions advance to `sub-bar-v22` and `local-study-v28` so old results are not silently reused after candidate-list changes.

The new names are usable and do not regress this selected baseline. None of the five fixtures independently verifies a positive real-audio example of these new exact extensions, so their automatic recognition precision is **unmeasured**. Do not increase eligibility based on the same tuning windows; add checked simultaneous-note and timing examples from other recordings first. Existing altered-fifth ambiguity remains [separate open work](2026-10-16-altered-fifth-reference.md).
